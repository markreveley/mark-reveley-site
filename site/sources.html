Business Process Model and Notation (Wikipedia)

Wikipedia contributors · living document · fetched

The workflow-engine era's graph notation — activities, gateways, events; BPMN 2.0 released January 2011; executed by engines like Activiti (2010) and jBPM.

Added in research push #2 — the maintainer's "workflow engines in 2012" hypothesis, documented. BPMN 2.0 (January 2011) standardized executable process graphs: activities (work), gateways (routing), events (triggers), swimlanes (roles), and crucially human tasks — interpretive nodes, at human speed. Engines of the 2010–2013 wave (Activiti, initial release December 2010, by ex-jBPM architects; jBPM; later Camunda) executed these graphs directly. The genealogy claim this supports: agent graph engineering descends from business process orchestration (which always mixed deterministic steps with judgment-bearing ones) at least as directly as from data-pipeline DAGs.
