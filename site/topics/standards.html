A corpus continuously maintained by agents

"Increasingly, a knowledge corpus is not authored once and then read: it is continuously written and maintained by agents."

[problem] — the five questions that follow:

"When most concepts are machine-generated, a consumer needs answers that a plain markdown-plus-frontmatter convention does not make first-class: 1. What was this created from, and how was it verified? (provenance) 2. How much should I trust it? (trust) 3. Is it still true? (freshness) 4. Is it the current version? (lifecycle) 5. Was this number produced the way we said it must be? (attestation)"

Quoted directly from the cloned spec (commit 9a15b13). The world-state the spec assumes, plus its five trust questions — machinery this bundle itself uses.
