Seek and Document Confusion

6 November 2026

AI is like fame or money. It's an enabler of delusion and wishful thinking. All three forces remove limits, and without limits creativity dies. When creativity dies, sanity follows. Neurosis is a product of control. You can have too much.

Confusion has value it did not before. Solutions are now commoditized. The qualification of solutions is tied to perspective, which is commodity resistant. Confusion is the predecessor of understanding, and understanding breeds perspective. Documenting the human struggle to understand has narrative and instructional value. Therefore documenting confusion has value.

Confusion lies at the frontier of understanding. The LLM allays confusion. That's its job. It doesn't extend the frontier of comprehension, that's not possible. Instead it renders a fantastic calibration level that allows for seemingly flawless delegation. Confusion is offloaded. In the beginning.

The big gamble we are seeing is related to how far out you can push confusion. Can you offload it completely? Can you speak of every domain and every intention from a high level perspective of simple outputs? "Do my taxes." "Buy flights on this day." "Get me a job." "Start a company." "Build me a perfect copy of Amazon.com." "Win me the election." If you are clear on intent, statements at this level are beyond the confusion that implementation brings. If you can trust your team, human or agentic, to perfectly execute your high level request, you can successfully function at this level. Tellingly, it seems that most cannot on this pure level. The world is a dynamic place, and the devil is in the details.

Before, I talked of the three forces that enable delusion, wishful thinking and neurosis. I conveniently left out power, the underlying substrate of all three. The force made explicit in a hierarchy.

An organization is a complex interconnected web of confusion processing entities. Where confusion needs to live for each depends on their role. The purpose of hierarchy is to defer and outsource confusion to those below you.

AI is allowing for the complete rupturing of the confusion calibration lattice of hierarchy. Great networks of knowledge workers have collapsed into strange new dimensions of semantic expression bundled with near instant execution.

The problem with what people are going to build, is the same as it's always been. What people are going to build.

Documenting confusion is now a valuable act. It's an act of epistemic rebellion. It's where creativity lives. It's admitting that we don't know. It's admitting that being able to do is not the same as understanding, when doing itself is increasingly delegation. It's choosing to return to a state that AI seeks to relieve you of. It forces you to transparently think. It's as irrational as it is effective.
