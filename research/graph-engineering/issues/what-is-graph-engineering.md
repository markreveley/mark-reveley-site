# Issue

Within 48 hours of the July 2026 naming event the term carried three competing meanings ([twelve words](../excerpts/aio--twelve-words.md)). The definitional positions on record differ on what nodes are (knowledge vs. capability), who designs the paths, and where the knowledge lives.

# Positions on record

* [A graph is two things: nodes and edges](../excerpts/aio--nodes-and-edges.md) - The field guide's buzzword-free minimal definition, cast in knowledge terms — the things you know about, and the connections between them.
* [An untyped edge is one bit](../excerpts/aio--typed-edges-one-bit.md) - The typed-edge distinction — "related" carries one bit of information; supersedes / depends_on / decided_by / caused carry the meaning.
* [The edge type IS the knowledge](../excerpts/ft--edge-type-is-knowledge.md) - The maximal typed-edge thesis — relatedness is trivial to detect; the typed edge is what answers "why did this change?
* [Explicit graphs an agent can traverse](../excerpts/ft--explicit-graphs-definition.md) - Flowtivity's definition uniting both senses — entities, decisions, and concepts as nodes; typed edges; traversal by the agent — plus the concurrency argument against loops.
* [The AI moves within a pre-designed map](../excerpts/gd--pre-designed-map.md) - Gao Dalie's determinist definition — humans design the objectives, criteria, and the entire path; the AI does not wander.
* [Boring nodes, typed edges, checkpointed state](../excerpts/js--nodes-edges-state.md) - Simmons' three commitments — the definitional core of graph engineering in its orchestration sense.
* [This week's name for an orchestrated system of loops](../excerpts/lb--this-weeks-name.md) - Bouchard's deflationary definition — graph engineering as the newest label on a rename treadmill, for a practice that already existed.
* [A map of how something works](../excerpts/rl--map-metaphor.md) - The commenter's closing distillation — the graph is a map you create of how something works, then read back.
