# Issues

Root nodes of the corpus DAG (v3, IBIS-hybrid): the questions the position excerpts respond to. Each issue lists its positions on record; arguments and evidence reach issues through the positions they support or object to. Issues state questions only — per the house rule, no issue node takes a side.

* [Is it just fancier retrieval?](is-it-just-retrieval.md) - Whether the graph trend reduces to a retrieval upgrade, or changes what is stored, who maintains it, and what else the structure governs.
* [What is “graph engineering”?](what-is-graph-engineering.md) - The contested definition — knowledge sense, orchestration sense, both, or merely a new name.
* [Loops versus graphs](loops-vs-graphs.md) - The relation between loop engineering and graph engineering — succession, containment, or identity.
* [How much structure?](how-much-structure.md) - How much explicit, pre-designed structure an agent system should carry — the determinism–autonomy axis.
* [Paradigm or hype?](paradigm-or-hype.md) - Whether graph engineering is a genuinely new paradigm, a hype event, or a renaming of older practice.
* [One graph or two?](one-graph-or-two.md) - Whether the knowledge graph and the execution graph are, or should become, the same object.
* [Possible with LangGraph?](possible-with-langgraph.md) - Whether graph-engineering practice is practicable in LangGraph today, and what remains missing.
