# Graph Engineering — Research Bundle

A research bundle on **graph engineering**: the term's historical senses and its 2026 emergence as the successor/companion to *loop engineering* in AI-agent practice. Compiled 2026-08-24. See [README](README.md) for method, format decisions, and open questions.

# Guide

* [README](README.md) - How this bundle is organized, the OKF conventions and extension fields it uses, the excerpt subtype enum, the tag taxonomy, and open questions for the maintainer.
* [Update log](log.md) - Chronological history of changes to this bundle.

# Level 1 — Sources

* [References](references/index.md) - Annotated registry of primary and secondary sources, one concept per source, with availability and verification notes. Includes a further-reading list of sources not yet decomposed.

# Level 2 — Excerpts

* [Excerpts](excerpts/index.md) - Verbatim quotes, one concept per unit, each carrying a `role` in the dialectic (issue / position / argument / evidence — the v3 IBIS-hybrid facet) and a `subtype` speech-act flavor, tagged and cross-linked with typed `deps` forming a verified DAG.
* [Issues](issues/index.md) - The seven root questions the position excerpts respond to; each lists its positions on record and takes no side.

# Level 3 — Views

* [Views](views/index.md) - Non-narrative compositions over the quote DAG; currently the [timeline](views/timeline.md).
