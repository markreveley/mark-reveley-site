# About

Added in research push #2 — three weeks after the naming event, the vendor's own answer to "is the new paradigm possible with LangGraph?" Their stack is a spectrum: Deep Agents (harness, model decides), LangChain (loop in the middle), LangGraph (maximal determinism, topology encodes domain knowledge). Two things follow for this bundle's questions: LangGraph is presented as *sufficient* for the determinist end of graph engineering, and — notably — as the *last* resort, not the default: "start with Deep Agents."
