# About

Added in research push #2, with a double relevance. Genealogically: IBIS and gIBIS (Conklin & Begeman's 1988 hypertext implementation, built to capture design rationale as "typed IBIS networks") are the argumentation-graph ancestor of typed-edge knowledge graphs. Reflexively: this bundle's own excerpt schema — `question` / `claim` / `problem` / `solution` / typed `supports`·`contradicts` relations — is an extended IBIS (issues / positions / arguments) rediscovered under a different name, which the maintainer may want to weigh in the format discussion.
