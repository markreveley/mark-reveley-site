# About

Added in research push #2. Vendor-authored but factually specific: Maxim Fateev (Amazon from 2002; SWF) and Samar Abbas (Amazon → Microsoft, where the Durable Task Framework became Azure Durable Functions) reunited at Uber to build Cadence, then founded Temporal (2019) around "Durable Execution." This is the strongest personnel-continuity evidence in the bundle: the people who built the 2012 workflow engine are the people who built the durable-execution substrate that 2026 agent orchestration runs on — and Temporal now markets exactly that ([WorkOS interview with Fateev on durable execution for AI agents](https://workos.com/blog/maxim-fateev-temporal-durable-execution-ai-agents), April 2026; registered as further reading — no direct quotes available from the paraphrased writeup).
