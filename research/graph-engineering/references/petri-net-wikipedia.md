# About

Added in research push #2 (the paradigm-or-hype investigation). Carl Adam Petri's 1962 dissertation formalism is the deepest ancestor of graph-shaped *control flow*: concurrency, synchronization, and state as token flow over a typed bipartite graph — and via workflow nets (van der Aalst), the formal semantics underneath the 1990s–2010s workflow-engine wave.
