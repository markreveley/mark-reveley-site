# About

Bouchard treats the viral moment as a joke that "points at something real": the name is new, the practice is not (he credits Anthropic's 2024 *Building Effective Agents* with drawing "every one of these patterns"). His original contributions: what actually changed is that graph nodes now *interpret* their tasks rather than follow fixed rules; and the matching failure mode — "a graph of agents checking agents can produce extremely organized nonsense."
