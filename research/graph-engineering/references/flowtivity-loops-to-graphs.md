# About

Defines graph engineering as "designing AI systems around explicit graphs" with typed edges an agent can traverse; argues loops can't do concurrency; and pushes the strongest version of the typed-edge thesis ("the edge type IS the knowledge"). Carries its own caveats: pass-rate thresholds for when graphs win on cost, and the same per-hop decay arithmetic as [the field guide](aioperator-field-guide.md).
