# Arguments (10)

* [The decision lives in the structure](the-decision-lives-in-the-structure.md) - The field guide's case against similarity search — the ten most similar chunks cannot explain a decision whose meaning is carried by relationships. *(problem)*

* [The constraint moved to coordination](the-constraint-moved-to-coordination.md) - Why now — model capability moved the binding constraint from step competence to system coordination, and coordination is a graph problem. *(inference)*
* [Three ceilings of the loop](three-ceilings-of-the-loop.md) - Simmons' bill of particulars against the single agent loop — serial execution, transcript-as-state, and no pause button. *(problem)*
* [Organized nonsense at industrial scale](organized-nonsense-at-industrial-scale.md) - The graph era's characteristic failure mode — agent nodes interpret rather than execute, so a graph of agents checking agents can compound error with perfect structure. *(problem)*

* [Decision trees don't fit CSV rows](decision-trees-dont-fit-csv-rows.md) - The concrete failure case — decision structures for 10,000 behaviors can be flattened into linked rows, but reading and retrieval degrade because the encoding fights the shape of the data. *(problem)*
