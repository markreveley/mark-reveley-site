# Analysis

The bundle's deepest root, kept for what Euler's move actually was — the template every graph argument since has repeated: **delete the territory, keep the map**. Landmasses and bridges become nodes and edges; everything about Königsberg except its connectivity is discarded; and the question becomes answerable *because of* what was thrown away. That is the [seed thread's abstraction-layer claim](rl--abstraction-layer.md), 290 years early, and the reason "you're creating [a map](rl--map-metaphor.md) of how something works" is not a loose metaphor but a description of the method. The chemistry note marks the other recurring pattern: graphs enter each era's *engineering* through whatever that era most needs to structure — molecules (1860s), circuits (Kirchhoff, 1840s), computation ([Pregel](pre--vertex-centric.md), 2010), and now agent systems. Dating note: Britannica says 1735 (Euler's presentation), Wikipedia 1736 (the paper) — per-source provenance preserved in the [reference](../references/wikipedia-seven-bridges.md).
