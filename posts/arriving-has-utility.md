Ai creates plausible arbitrary results. Using ai for synthesis creates arbitrary synthesis. It is useful as a proposed model of a synthesis. It is damaging for the same reason. The artifact of synthesis, the statement or claim, the arrival, is not the process of having arrived there. The process of arriving can have romantic value in some domains. In others it is the actual value. That is what we’re seeing emerge. AI is a forcing function and sorting mechanism. We begin to see what innately human created value is in a way we could not before
