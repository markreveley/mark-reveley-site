In the time before AI agents, philosophy occupied a particular niche. In some ways, it was computer science without the consequences. 

In the time of AI agents, consequence has returned to philosophy. What a human expresses matters. How an agent understands it matters. How an agent expresses matters. How a human understands it matters.

Many to most issues in agentic issues stem from insufficiently defined or under-specified statements.

Philosophy is the study of specification. And we now live in the time of specification.

So, what does philosophy have to say about what a proposition is? 

As it turns out, it has a lot to say. And as it also turns out...it depends.
