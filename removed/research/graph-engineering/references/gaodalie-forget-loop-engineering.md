# About

Argues graph engineering is about designing the relationships between processes: humans design the entire path, and the AI "moves within a pre-designed map" — an unwitting echo of the [seed thread's](reddit-llmdevs-graph-trend.md) map metaphor from the opposite direction (there, the map is *read* by the system; here, it *constrains* the system). Also the cleanest statement that loops nest inside graph nodes, and a warning against graphing everything.
