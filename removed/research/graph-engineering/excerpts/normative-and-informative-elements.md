# Quotes

> "3.2.1 normative element: element that describes the scope of the document (3.1.1) or sets out provisions" [^iso]

> "3.2.2 informative element: element intended to assist the understanding or use of the document (3.1.1) or that provides contextual information about its content, background or relationship with other documents" [^iso]

# Note

Clause 3.2, "Elements of a document," in the directive that governs how every ISO and IEC standard is structured and drafted. The distinction is load-bearing: which elements of a standard a claim of conformance must satisfy (normative), and which exist only to help a reader understand or apply it (informative) — including scope, foreword, and notes.

[^iso]: ISO/IEC Directives, Part 2 (8th edition, 2018), clause 3.2
